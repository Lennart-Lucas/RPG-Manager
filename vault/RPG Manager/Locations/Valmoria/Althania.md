# Althania

> *There is no crown in Althania. There is a crosier, and it is heavier.*
> — Althan proverb, recorded for correction

Althania is the realm the Rising built: nine hundred and fifty-eight years of one God, one book, and one very long argument about who speaks for both. It is not a kingdom. It is a communion of some three hundred jurisdictions — prince-bishoprics, abbey-lands, sworn baronies, chartered towns, mining liberties, and a dozen places whose legal status has been under review since the four-hundreds — held together by the [[Church of Orzhova]] and by the certainty that anything which comes apart will be put back together by [[Althania's Holy Inquisition|the Inquisition]].

**At a glance.** Some four million souls. Ruled by **Hierarch Vasmund III**, elected 944 AR. Capital [[Weissturm]]. The year is **958 AR** — *Anno Resurgentis*, reckoned from the Rising. Tongue: Althan in the mouth, Ecclesiast on the page.

## The Shape of the Realm

A high country of ridges and river valleys, fed by silver and choked by forest. The spine of it is the **Aschen range**, whose mines have paid for every cathedral in the realm and buried the men who dug them. West of the mountains the land falls into wool country and terraced vineyards; east it breaks into the **Tern basin**, wide, wealthy, and disloyal. North of the ridge-line the settlements thin into the **marches** — border country, oak forest, and old stone that nobody built.

Two forests matter. The [[Dornwald]], which the Church says is empty. The [[Knarrholt|Creakwood]], which the Church says the same thing about, with less conviction.

Roads are Church roads, kept by abbey levy, and every crossroads carries a post with the unbroken ring cut into it. Where the rings stop, the marches begin.

## Who Rules

**The Hierarch** — *Vicar Undivided, Bishop of [[Weissturm]], First Voice of the Concord, Keeper of the Whiteroot Stone.* Elected for life. Cannot be deposed, tried, or contradicted, though three have been poisoned and one drowned in four inches of holy water. His authority is total in theory and, in practice, precisely as long as his reach: a Hierarch who has lost the Third Sacrament has lost the money, and a Hierarch who has lost the Second has lost the army.

**The Seven Crosiers** — the Elector-Bishops, who rule as princes and vote as electors.

| See | Holds | Temper |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [[Salvamont]] | The birthplace of the faith, the pilgrim roads, the great silver | Rich, sanctimonious, and permanently in debt to its own splendour |
| **Aschenberg** | The mountain mines, the powder mills, the memory of the Grey Communion | Grim, watchful, thoroughly infiltrated by the Sixth |
| **Hohenfurt** | The northern marches and their fortified abbeys | Militant. Its bishops fight, and several have died doing it |
| **Lucerna** | The impression engines, the schools, the archives | Learned, insufferable, and the First Sacrament's home ground |
| **Ternovum** | The Tern basin, the river trade, the near bank of the Schism | Mercantile, compromised, watched |
| **Veldenau** | Grain, wool, and the largest peasantry in the realm | Quiet since 418 AR, and pointedly so |
| **Rabenmark** | The eastern approaches, the fortresses, the war | Poor, armed, and owed a great deal of money |

**The Concord** — the diet of the realm, meeting at Weissturm when summoned: electors, bishops, mitred abbots, the sworn houses, and the burgomasters of the chartered cities, each convinced the other three benches are the problem. It votes taxes, wars, and canon. It has never once concluded in under six weeks.

**The Sworn Houses** — the secular nobility. They hold land by *investiture*: a bishop places the ring, and the house swears. A house that displeases its bishop can be un-invested, which is legally elegant and has started nine wars.

## The Election

When a Hierarch dies, the seven electors are sealed into the Chapter House at Weissturm and are not fed well. Ballots are burned in the **Ash Font**. Black smoke, no Hierarch; white smoke, a Hierarch.

Nobody is permitted to observe the count. Everybody observes the smoke. Since 311 AR the [[The 3rd Sacrament - The Stewards of Providence]] have audited every elector's household for the twelvemonth preceding a conclave, a practice dating from the **Bought Conclave** of 306 that has not once, in six hundred years, found anything.

## Faith and Fear

The Church is not a department of the realm; the realm is a department of the Church. Every birth, marriage, oath, contract, will, and death passes through a parish register. The tithe is one part in ten, the **Ember Levy** is whatever the Concord votes in a war year, and **remissions** — written pardons against penance owed — are sold openly, priced by sin, and receipted in triplicate.

Above and outside all of it sits the Sixfold Holy Inquisition: six orders, six divisions to be healed, answerable to the Hierarch and to nobody else. Ordinary Althanians will deal with the Second in the field, the Third at the toll-house, and the Fifth on the worst night of their lives. They hope never to learn what the Sixth looks like, and the point of the Sixth is that they wouldn't.

## Coin, Powder and Trade

Silver comes out of the Aschen range and goes into cathedrals, war, and the vaults of the **Providence Houses**, the Stewards' banks, which lend to bishops at rates a merchant would call usury and a Steward calls stewardship.

Black powder is a Church monopoly, root and branch. Saltpetre is imported under seal, milled at Aschenberg and two lesser sites, and issued against a ledger the Third Sacrament reconciles four times a year. Cannon are cast by licensed foundries; handgonnes are numbered. A sworn house may keep sixty men-at-arms and as many crossbows as it pleases, and may not keep one barrel of powder it did not sign for. This single arrangement has done more to keep the nobility obedient than four centuries of sermons.

## The Host

There is no royal army. When the Concord calls the **Host**, the sees levy: perhaps sixty thousand at full call, of which a third will actually arrive. Its permanent parts are the **Cathedral Guard** of Weissturm, three thousand strong; the fortress garrisons of Hohenfurt and Rabenmark; and the **sworn bands**, professional companies that sell themselves abroad in peacetime and come home under Church contract in war.

Every company above two hundred men carries a Shepherd. He does not command. He decides whether the war is holy, and he decides it again every morning.

## Beyond the Borders

- **East, past the Tern:** the **Sundered Communion** — free cities that renounced Weissturm in 872 AR and kept the faith while rejecting the Hierarch. Not heathens; something far worse. The war with them is cold in most years and hot in some.
- **North, past the marches:** clan country, where the Nine Names were never quite pried loose, and where the oaks are still tall.
- **South and over water:** the trading powers, the grain that keeps Althania fed, and the ports through which saltpetre and rumours arrive from [[Wessarin]].

## The Realm in 958 AR

- **Hierarch Vasmund III is dying.** He is seventy-five, has not been seen at a public rite in eleven months, and the Curia issues his decrees in a hand that is not his. Every elector has begun spending.
- **The realm is in debt** from the **Penitent Campaign** of 951–955, which achieved nothing east of the Tern except the loss of an army and the reputation of two sees. The Stewards hold the paper on five of the seven electoral sees.
- **The Coughing Winter** of 956 emptied villages in Veldenau and Rabenmark. Landlords are enclosing the vacancies. The countryside is quietly furious in the way it was quietly furious in the year 0.
- **The marches are worse than they were.** The Fifth Sacrament has filed for more Wardens every year for a decade and been refused, because the Sixth's foreign work costs more each year and there is one purse.
- **Something is being tolerated.** Little cults sprout in the emptied villages and are stamped out late, by hunters trained for beasts rather than men. The Fifth says the Sixth is looking the wrong way. The Sixth does not answer the Fifth.

## Errors Commonly Repeated

- That the Concord could refuse a Hierarch's decree, if it ever agreed on anything.
- That the powder ledgers of Aschenberg have been short since the Penitent Campaign, and that nobody dares publish the reconciliation.
- That the Nine Names are still said in the north, in daylight, with the priest present.
- That the Ash Font at Weissturm has burned nothing for three conclaves, and that the smoke is made.
