> *There is no crown in Althania. There is a crosier, and it is heavier.*
> — Althan proverb, recorded for correction

Althania is the realm the Rising built: one God, one book, and one very long argument about who speaks for both. It is not a kingdom. It is a communion of some three hundred jurisdictions — prince-bishoprics, abbey-lands, sworn baronies, chartered towns, mining liberties, and a dozen places whose legal status has been under review since the four-hundreds — held together by the [[RPG Manager/Organisations/Church of Orzhova]] and by the certainty that anything which comes apart will be put back together by [[RPG Manager/Organisations/Church of Orzhova/Althania's Holy Inquisition|the Inquisition]].

## The Shape of the Realm
A country of river plains and rising country, hemmed on three sides by things that are not human.
Within Althania's own bounds:
- **The heartland** — the plains of the Elve and the Rill, grain and wool and the great majority of the realm's four million, with [[RPG Manager/Locations/Valmoria/Althania/Lucerna]] on the flat ground where the two rivers meet.
- **The western uplands** — the country of [[RPG Manager/Locations/Valmoria/Althania/Salvamont]], its lonely hill, its silver, and the fen and forest-edge below it, where [[RPG Manager/Locations/Valmoria/Althania/Dunvicum]] and [[RPG Manager/Locations/Valmoria/Althania/Knarrholt]] sit.
- **The northern marches** — foothill country climbing toward the mountains, thin, poor, and full of fortified abbeys. It ends at [[RPG Manager/Locations/Valmoria/Althania/Weissturm]].
- **The [[RPG Manager/Locations/Valmoria/Althania/Dornwald]]** — in the east, forty miles of black thorn and dead oak grown over the ground where the last priests of the Nine Names were burned. It is Althanian on every map and entered by nobody; its outer half-mile is cut by the woodcutters of [[RPG Manager/Locations/Valmoria/Althania/Eichenwacht]].
- **The Tern basin** — the south-east: wide, wealthy, river-fed and reliably disloyal.
- **The southern coast** — the ports through which the realm eats.

## What Borders It
- **North and north-east — the World Spine Mountains**, and the dwarven thanedoms beneath them. Not Althanian ground. The one place the border is a city rather than a line is [[RPG Manager/Locations/Valmoria/Althania/Weissturm]], which is Althanian above and dwarven below by the Concord of Stone, and which holds the hard high passes.
- **Beyond the Spine — Shall**, the dark elf city. Two wars, and more raids than the Chronicle bothers to list. The easy way through the mountains is the Low Pass, and the first walled thing on that road is [[RPG Manager/Locations/Valmoria/Althania/Salvamont]]. There is a truce. Nobody calls it a peace.
- **West — the Blackwood.** Old forest, claimed entire by the wood elves who live in it. Althania cuts its outermost fringe under a charter they have never acknowledged and has never been told to stop, which the Concord takes for consent and the woodcutters do not.
- **East and south-east, past the Tern — the Sundered Communion.** Free cities that renounced the Hierarch and kept the faith. Not heathens; something far worse.

## Who Rules
* **The Hierarch** — *Vicar Undivided, Bishop of Lucerna, First Voice of the Concord, Keeper of the Whiteroot Stone.* Elected for life. Cannot be deposed, tried, or contradicted, though three have been poisoned and one drowned in four inches of holy water. His authority is total in theory and, in practice, precisely as long as his reach: a Hierarch who has lost the Third Sacrament has lost the money, and a Hierarch who has lost the Second has lost the army. The last of his styles is an embarrassment nobody will drop. The Whiteroot Stone is at Weissturm, in a tower he does not command, over roots that run down into a thanedom that is not his.
* **The Seven Crosiers** — the Elector-Bishops, who rule as princes and vote as electors. Lucerna holds no vote, because its bishop counts the ballots.

* **The Concord** — the diet of the realm, meeting at Lucerna when summoned: electors, bishops, mitred abbots, the sworn houses, and the burgomasters of the chartered cities, each convinced the other three benches are the problem. It votes taxes, wars, and canon. It has never once concluded in under six weeks.
* **The Sworn Houses** — the secular nobility. They hold land by *investiture*: a bishop places the ring, and the house swears. A house that displeases its bishop can be un-invested, which is legally elegant and has started nine wars.

## The Election
When a Hierarch dies, the seven electors are sealed into the Chapter House at Lucerna and are not fed well. Ballots are burned in the **Ash Font**. Black smoke, no Hierarch; white smoke, a Hierarch.
Nobody is permitted to observe the count. Everybody observes the smoke. Since 311 AR [[RPG Manager/Organisations/Church of Orzhova/Althania's Holy Inquisition/The 3rd Sacrament - The Stewards of Providence|The Third]] have audited every elector's household for the twelvemonth preceding a conclave — a practice dating from the **Bought Conclave** of 306 that has never yet found anything.

## Faith and Fear
The Church is not a department of the realm; the realm is a department of the Church. Every birth, marriage, oath, contract, will, and death passes through a parish register. The tithe is one part in ten, the **Ember Levy** is whatever the Concord votes in a war year, and **remissions** — written pardons against penance owed — are sold openly, priced by sin, and receipted in triplicate.
Above and outside all of it sits the [[RPG Manager/Organisations/Church of Orzhova/Althania's Holy Inquisition|Sixfold Holy Inquisition]]: six orders, six divisions to be healed, answerable to the Hierarch and to nobody else. Ordinary Althanians will deal with the Second in the field, the Third at the toll-house, and the Fifth on the worst night of their lives. They hope never to learn what the Sixth looks like, and the point of the Sixth is that they wouldn't.

## Magic
Arcane power is not grace and is not heresy. Doctrine holds it a property of creation, like fire — and the whole of the offence is leaving it unattended. So every caster in the realm is found young, registered by their curate, and taken north to the **White Tower** at Weissturm, where they are trained, sealed, entered on the Roll of Sanctioned Grace, and mostly never leave. A caster off the Roll is **unwritten**, and the unwritten are hunted by [[RPG Manager/Organisations/Church of Orzhova/Althania's Holy Inquisition/The 6th Sacrament - The Keepers of the Last Flame|The Sixth]].

## Coin, Powder and Trade
Silver comes out of the hills and goes into cathedrals, war, and the vaults of the **Providence Houses**, the Stewards' banks, which lend to bishops at rates a merchant would call usury and a Steward calls stewardship.
Black powder is a Church monopoly, root and branch. Saltpetre is imported under seal, milled at Aschenberg and two lesser sites, and issued against a ledger the Third reconciles four times a year. Cannon are cast by licensed foundries; handgonnes are numbered. A sworn house may keep sixty men-at-arms and as many crossbows as it pleases, and may not keep one barrel of powder it did not sign for. This single arrangement has done more to keep the nobility obedient than four centuries of sermons.

## The Host
There is no royal army. When the Concord calls the **Host**, the sees levy: perhaps sixty thousand at full call, of which a third will actually arrive. Its permanent parts are the **Cathedral Guard** of Lucerna, three thousand strong; the fortress garrisons of Hohenfurt and Rabenmark; and the **sworn bands**, professional companies that sell themselves abroad in peacetime and come home under Church contract in war. Every company above two hundred men carries a Shepherd. He does not command. He decides whether the war is holy, and he decides it again every morning.
And beneath [[RPG Manager/Locations/Valmoria/Althania/Salvamont]], in galleries that are officially worked out, stands [[RPG Manager/Organisations/The Black Legion]], which does not exist and has twice been the only reason the realm still has a west.

## As Matters Stand (958 AR)
- **Hierarch Vasmund III is dying.** He is seventy-five, has not been seen at a public rite in eleven months, and the Curia issues his decrees in a hand that is not his. Every elector has begun spending.
- **The realm is in debt** from the **Penitent Campaign** of 951–955, which achieved nothing east of the Tern except the loss of an army and the reputation of two sees. The Stewards hold the paper on five of the seven electoral sees.
- **The Coughing Winter** of 956 emptied villages in Veldenau and Rabenmark. Landlords are enclosing the vacancies. The countryside is quietly furious in the way it was quietly furious in the year 0.
- **The truce with Shall is fraying.** Raids in the Low Pass foothills are up, and the Legion has lost sleepers to four incidents the see of Salvamont will not describe.
- **The Blackwood is closing.** Woodcutters out of Knarrholt find their cut rides grown over faster than a season can explain, and Althanian claim to the fringe rests on a charter the wood elves have never once acknowledged.
- **The marches are worse than they were.** The Fifth Sacrament has asked for more Wardens every year for a decade and been refused, because the Sixth's foreign work costs more each year and there is one purse.
