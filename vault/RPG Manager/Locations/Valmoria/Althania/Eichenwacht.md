# Eichenwacht

> *The oak watch. Nobody in the village can tell you what it was watching, and every one of them will tell you it was not the road.*

A border village in the northern marches on the ridge road toward the [[RPG Manager/Locations/Valmoria/Althania/Dornwald]], eleven miles from the iron stakes. The Church name is **Eichenwacht**, the Oak Watch. The older name, still used by the family that holds the manor and by anyone over sixty, is **Velmorne**.

**At a glance.** 310 people, down from 540 two winters ago. A sworn manor of House Velmorne under the see of Hohenfurt. The baron is nine years old.

## The Watch-Tower

Stone, squat, and older than the Rising. The Church's account is that it was a beacon-post of the Crowned Houses. It stands on the ridge in a ring of oaks that were not planted in any pattern a forester recognises, and the tower's single window looks north, at the wood, and at nothing on the road at all.

House Velmorne has held it for four hundred years and has kept a man in it, by custom, every night of every one of them. Nobody, including the Velmornes, can produce a document explaining why.

## The Village

Oak forest, pannage pigs, thin wool. The great oaks are under restriction — a warrant is needed to fell one, which the villagers regard as the Church at last making sense about something.

There is one church, one curate (Father Emrich, thirty-one, sent here from Lucerna for reasons he does not discuss), one tavern, and a green with a stone at the middle of it that the curate has twice petitioned to have removed.

## The Velmornes

An old, poor, stubborn sworn house. The baron is nine. His uncle **Karl** holds the estate as Steward-Regent, and Karl is the sort of man who says very little and pays his levies late and exactly.

The family's private chapel is not consecrated to any saint in the Canon. It is dedicated to *the Watch*, which is not a thing, which is why nobody has ever been able to formulate a charge about it.

## In 958 AR

The Coughing Winter took two hundred and thirty people and the survivors buried them fast, in a single ground, without the full rite, because there were not hands enough. Father Emrich has been trying since to perform the rite properly over the ground and has been prevented — not violently, not once refused outright, simply prevented, week after week, by a village that is always busy.

Since the spring the man in the tower has been posted with a horn, which is new, and the horn has been sounded twice, and both times nothing came up the road.

Karl Velmorne has begun sending his nephew away for weeks at a time, to relatives he has never previously mentioned.

## Errors Commonly Repeated

- That the tower was built to watch the wood, and that the Velmornes were paid to do it by someone who has not stopped paying.
- That the stone on the green is the same kind of stone as the capped stumps inside the Dornwald.
- That the burial ground of 957 was not dug where the curate was told it was dug.
