> *They cut the oak down. They did not pull the roots out. You may draw your own conclusions, but not aloud.*

The capital of [[RPG Manager/Locations/Valmoria/Althania]] and the seat of the [[RPG Manager/Organisations/Church of Orzhova]], built in 9 AR on the stump of the **Whiteroot** — the white-barked oak of Aonir, oldest holy thing of the Diademed Age, felled and burned at the end of the Nine Years' Rising. The stump was capped in stone. The **White Tower** was raised over the cap. The Hierarch is styled *Keeper of the Whiteroot Stone*, and it is the only title of his that refers to a thing you can touch.
**At a glance.** 41,000 within the walls, some 15,000 more in the Undercrook. Held directly by the Hierarch as its bishop; run day to day by the Curia. Garrison: the Cathedral Guard, three thousand.

## The Shape of It
- **The Tower and the Crown Precinct** — the White Tower, the Chapter House, the Ash Font, the Curia's chanceries, and the sealed courts of [[RPG Manager/Organisations/Althania's Holy Inquisition]]. Walled within the city. Admission by writ.
- **The Nine Steps** — the processional stair from the lower city, one landing for each year of the Rising. Every pilgrim climbs it. Every decree is read from it.
- **The Ledgerhouse** — the Providence House of the [[RPG Manager/Organisations/Althania's Holy Inquisition/The 3rd Sacrament - The Stewards of Providence|Third Sacrament]], a windowless block of grey stone that is quietly the second most important building in the realm.
- **The Scribes' Quarter** — two of the eleven impression engines, the guild of copyists, and more taverns per street than anywhere in Althania, because clerks drink.
- **The Undercrook** — the sprawl outside the walls: petitioners, litigants, pilgrims, ruined claimants, and the families of men whose cases have been pending for a generation. Some households have been petitioning for four.

## What Happens Here
Nothing is decided quickly in Weissturm, and everything is decided in Weissturm. A dispute over a mill-race in the far marches will, if pursued, end in a room in the Crown Precinct in front of a man who has never seen a mill.
The city runs on **writs, seals and precedence**. Which of two men enters a door first is a matter of record, and there is an office that keeps it.

## The Conclave
When a Hierarch dies, the seven electors are sealed into the **Chapter House**, fed thin, and left. Ballots burn in the **Ash Font**; black smoke, no Hierarch; white smoke, a Hierarch. The city fills weeks in advance — banners, bought lodgings, sworn bands hired as "retinue", and every Sacrament's people in every inn. The Chapter House has been swept three times this year. The Font has been inspected twice. Nobody has said why.

## In 958 AR
Vasmund III has not been seen at a public rite since last autumn. Chancellor **Aldric Menn** issues his decrees, in his name, in a hand that is demonstrably not his, and no one has yet been willing to be the first to say so in a room with a Recorder in it.
The Undercrook is fuller than it has been in living memory. The bread price has doubled. And a great many well-dressed men have arrived from the east with no stated business.
