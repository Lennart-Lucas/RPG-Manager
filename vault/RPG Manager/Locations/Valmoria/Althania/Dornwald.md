# Dornwald

> *Entry to the Dornwald is not forbidden. Forbidding a thing implies that people are attempting it.*
> — standing order of the Fifth Sacrament, 953 AR

Black thorn and dead oak in the eastern marches, forty miles across, grown over the ground where the last priesthoods of the Nine Names were burned in 8 AR.

Its outer half-mile is worked. The woodcutters of [[Eichenwacht]] take thorn billets for charcoal and standing deadwood for timber, always within sight of the iron stakes, always two to a tree, always stopping at dusk wherever they happen to be. Past that fringe nobody goes — not because a writ forbids it, but because nobody ever has come back from further in with a story anyone wanted to hear twice.

## The Season of Teeth

In 563 AR something came out of it. Over eight winters forty-one villages emptied — not sacked; **emptied**, doors shut, tables laid, livestock in the byres. Two levies marched east and did not come back in any number worth counting.

[[The 5th Sacrament - The Wardens of the Argent Hunt]] was founded in 570 to end it. The Season ended in 571. The order has never once claimed it was responsible, which is not modesty; the Fifth claims everything it kills, in writing, in the Ledger of the Circuit, and the entry for 571 is blank.

## The Boundary

A line of iron stakes, one every thirty paces, maintained without a break since 572 AR by the see of Rabenmark at a cost that appears in the Concord's accounts every single year under a heading nobody reads aloud. Nine march-posts garrison it. Their standing orders are to watch outward from the wood, and to record.

The stakes are replaced on a fixed rotation. Roughly one in forty is found already replaced.

## Unentered

Since 953 AR no writ has been issued for the Dornwald. No Warden may enter it. The order will not say who took that decision or on what evidence, and the Fifth is not an order that stops hunting things.

Before that the Wardens entered on average twice a decade. The Ledger entries for those expeditions are, without exception, short.

## What Is Known

- The thorn is black-barked, grows in rings rather than stands, and does not burn well on the stump even in a dry year — which was tested exhaustively in 566, 567 and 601. Cut and seasoned, it burns beautifully, which the powder mills have never stopped finding unsettling.
- Inside the outer mile there are stones: capped stumps, like the Whiteroot Stone at [[Weissturm]], but smaller and older and far more numerous.
- Compasses are reliable. Dogs are not. The argent hounds will work the boundary and will not cross it, and no Houndmaster has ever made one do so.
- There are four village sites within the wood that appear on no map, because they were struck from the register in 571. Their names are on the Silent Shelf at [[Lucerna]].

## As Matters Stand (958 AR)

The march-posts have filed eleven "irregularities" in two years, against a long-run average of about one. The see of Rabenmark has asked the Conclave twice for a Warden detachment on the boundary and been told the boundary is not a circuit.

Two Eichenwacht cutting gangs have come back short-handed this year, both from inside the stakes' sight-line, which had not happened before.

## Errors Commonly Repeated

- That the Season of Teeth was not ended but concluded, on terms.
- That the blank entry for 571 was not left blank but effaced, and that the Fifth did not do the effacing.
- That the stakes are not there to keep men out.
- That the reason nobody may enter is not that they would not come out.
