> *Entry to the Dornwald is not forbidden. Forbidding a thing implies that people are attempting it.*
> — Standing order of the [[RPG Manager/Organisations/Church of Orzhova/Althania's Holy Inquisition/The 5th Sacrament - The Wardens of the Argent Hunt|Fifth Sacrament]], 953 AR

Forty miles of black thorn and dead oak lie across the eastern marches of Althania, and no map drawn since the Rising shows a road through them. This is the Dornwald. In the eighth year after the Rising the last priesthoods of the Nine Names were driven into these trees and burned there, and the wood that grew up out of that ash has been let alone ever since.

The outer half-mile is worked. The woodcutters of [[RPG Manager/Locations/Valmoria/Althania/Eichenwacht]] take thorn billets for charcoal and standing deadwood for timber, always within sight of the iron stakes, always two men to a tree, always stopping at dusk wherever they happen to be standing. Past that fringe nobody goes. No writ forbids the ordinary man, and no writ needs to.

## The Season of Teeth

In 563 AR something came out of the wood. Over eight winters forty-one villages emptied, and the word the registers use is *emptied* rather than sacked: doors shut, tables laid, livestock still in the byres. Two levies marched east against it and did not come back in any number worth counting.

[[RPG Manager/Organisations/Church of Orzhova/Althania's Holy Inquisition/The 5th Sacrament - The Wardens of the Argent Hunt|The Fifth Sacrament]] was founded in 570 to end the Season, and the Season ended the following year. The order has never once claimed responsibility for that, which is not modesty. The Fifth claims everything it kills, in writing, in the Ledger of the Circuit, and the entry for 571 is blank.

## The Boundary

A line of iron stakes runs around the wood, one every thirty paces, maintained without a break since 572 AR by the see of Rabenmark. Its cost appears in the Concord's accounts every single year under a heading that nobody reads aloud. Nine march-posts garrison the line, and their standing orders are to watch outward from the trees and to write down what they see.

The stakes are replaced on a fixed rotation. Roughly one in forty is found to have been replaced already, by hands that filed no return, and the march-posts have been instructed to note the fact and not to investigate it.

## Unentered

Since 953 AR no writ has been issued for the Dornwald and no Warden may enter it. The order will not say who took that decision, or on what evidence, and the Fifth is not an order that stops hunting things.

Before the standing order the Wardens went in on average twice a decade. The Ledger entries for those expeditions are, without exception, short.

## What Is Known

The thorn is black-barked and grows in rings rather than in stands. On the stump it will not burn even in a dry year, which was established exhaustively in 566, 567 and 601. Cut and seasoned it burns beautifully, and the powder mills have never entirely stopped finding that unsettling.

Inside the outer mile there are stones — capped stumps, of the same kind as the Whiteroot Stone at [[RPG Manager/Locations/Valmoria/Althania/Weissturm]], but smaller and older and very much more numerous. Nobody has counted them, because counting them would mean going in to do it.

Compasses work normally in the Dornwald. Dogs do not. The argent hounds will work the boundary line all day and will not cross it, and no Houndmaster in the history of the order has managed to make one.

Four village sites stand within the wood which appear on no map, because they were struck from the register in 571 AR. Their names survive on the Silent Shelf beneath [[RPG Manager/Locations/Valmoria/Althania/Lucerna]], where only a Lector of the third degree may draw them, and where drawing them is recorded against his own name.

## As Matters Stand (958 AR)

The march-posts have filed eleven irregularities in the last two years, against a long-run average of about one. The see of Rabenmark has twice asked the Conclave for a Warden detachment on the boundary, and has twice been told that a boundary is not a circuit.

Two Eichenwacht cutting gangs came back short-handed this year, both of them from inside the stakes' sight-line, which had not happened before. The garrison of the fourth march-post says the stakes on their stretch are being replaced faster than the rotation accounts for, and that they have stopped writing down how much faster.
