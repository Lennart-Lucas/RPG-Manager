# Knarrholt

> *The wood does not creak in the wind. That is the first thing they tell you, and they tell you as if it were a joke.*

A timber and charcoal town on the northern edge of the **Creakwood**, and the seat of a chapter-house of [[RPG Manager/Organisations/Althania's Holy Inquisition/The 5th Sacrament - The Wardens of the Argent Hunt]]. It exists because the realm needs charcoal, and it survives because of the men in the long grey house at the top of the street.

**At a glance.** 1,900 in the town, three thousand more scattered across the charcoal camps. A charter town of the see of Hohenfurt, under Reeve Halma Storr — and, in every way that matters after dark, under Master of the Kill **Berent Ossel**.

## The Town

Wooden, wet, and dark by four in the afternoon for five months of the year. The charcoal camps run out into the forest on cut rides that are re-cut every spring because they close faster than they should. The **ash-lye** works stink. The **silver-fir** cut is reserved by warrant for gunstocks, and a Steward comes twice a year to count the trees.

Every byre door has a knot of straw over it. Every field has an uncut oak at its corner. The curate, Father Wend, is sixty and has stopped mentioning it.

## The Chapter-House

Long, low, grey, and the only stone building in Knarrholt. Inside: the kennels, the still-room, the armoury, and the **Ledger of the Circuit** — every kill, curse, haunt and blighted acre recorded since 574 AR, in the hands of two hundred dead Wardens.

The **argent hounds** are bred here. They do not bark. Townsfolk step off the road for them.

Ossel holds the circuit: nineteen Wardens for a hundred and forty miles of forest, which is not enough and has not been enough since he took the seat.

## The Creakwood

A forest of enormous, close-grown oak and fir with a floor of standing water. It does not creak in wind; it creaks in still air, at night, in a pattern the Wardens have been recording for two hundred years and have never published.

Three rules, taught to every child in Knarrholt before they are five:

1. Do not answer anything that calls your name from the trees.
2. Do not cut an oak that has a stone at its foot.
3. Come back before dark, or do not come back until light.

Nobody in the town regards these as superstitions. The Wardens do not correct them.

## In 958 AR

The circuit has lost **four Wardens in two years** — four times its usual rate — and Master of the Hunt Ottilie Reyn moved her own household to Knarrholt last spring, which Wardens do not do for ordinary bad luck.

Two of the four were lost at the same place, eleven months apart, on the ride toward the [[RPG Manager/Locations/Valmoria/Althania/Dornwald]] border.

The charcoal camps in the deep cut have taken to keeping a fire that is never allowed out, and to leaving a portion of every meal at the edge of the light. Ossel has been told. Ossel has filed it as *practice of uncertain origin, harmless*, which is a lie, and he knows what filing a lie makes him.

## Errors Commonly Repeated

- That the creaking is speech, and that the Ledger of the Circuit contains a transcription.
- That the stones at the feet of certain oaks were put there by the Wardens, not by the old faith.
- That Ossel keeps a cell in the chapter-house cellar that has been occupied continuously since before he was born.
