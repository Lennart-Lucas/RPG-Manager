This item can take the form of any instrument but always requires two hands to use. You can use your action to play it and draw mad inspiration from the demonic realms of chaos. When you do so, you must make a Charisma Saving Throw: DC 10.
**Success:** You regain one use of your Bardic Inspiration feature.
**Failure:** You gain one random Madness of Excess from the table below. If you roll a madness that you already have, reroll until you gain a new one. The madness lasts until you finish a long rest.
Each time you regain a use of Bardic Inspiration using this item, the save DC of subsequent uses increases by 2. The save DC resets to 10 at the next dawn.
If you have every madness effect from the Madness of Excess Effects table, you can't use this item until at least one of them has cleared.

1) **Avarice.** You cannot willingly let go of any objects. If an item is taken from you, you take 1 [[RPG Manager/Damage Types/Psychic]] damage.
2) **Carnality.** You are Charmed by all other creatures. If any creature harms you, you are no longer Charmed by that creature.
3) **Gluttony.** You must eat a day's worth of rations every 3 hours or suffer one level of exhaustion.
4) **Indolence.** You require 3 hours for a short rest and 24 hours for a long rest.
5) **Paramountcy.** You have disadvantage on all Wisdom ability checks.
6) **Vainglory.** Whenever you roll a natural 20 on a d20 test, treat it as if you rolled a 1.
