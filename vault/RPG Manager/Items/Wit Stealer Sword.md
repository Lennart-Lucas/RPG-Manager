This blade inflames the minds of creatures gripped by emotion, leaving them [[RPG Manager/Conditions/Dazed]] and overwhelmed by chaotic thoughts.
You gain a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this magic weapon, and attacks made with it deal an additional 1d6 [[RPG Manager/Damage Types/Psychic]] damage. If you hit a creature with this weapon that is [[RPG Manager/Conditions/Charmed]], [[RPG Manager/Conditions/Drowsy]], [[RPG Manager/Conditions/Frightened]], or [[RPG Manager/Conditions/Obsessed]], it is [[RPG Manager/Conditions/Dazed]] until the end of its next turn.
