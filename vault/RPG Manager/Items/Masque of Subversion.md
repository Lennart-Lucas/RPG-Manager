This hauntingly beautiful mask beguiles and bewitches those who would cause its wearer harm. While wearing this mask, if a creature you can see within 120 feet of you makes an attack roll against you, you can use your reaction to force the attacker must make a DC 20 Charisma saving throw. On a failed save, the attacker must target another creature within range of the attack other than you.
If multiple creatures are within range, the attacker chooses which one to target. If no other creatures are within range of the attack, the attack is wasted.
