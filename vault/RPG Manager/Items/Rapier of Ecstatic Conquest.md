This thin, elegant blade is engraved with thousands of whispering, red-lipped mouths, and it can bestow crippling bliss with the slightest scratch. Attacks with this rapier score a critical hit on an attack roll of 19 or 20. Additionally, creatures struck by a critical hit from this weapon are [[RPG Manager/Conditions/Drowsy]] until the end of your next turn.
