This entrancing gemstone can be incorporated into any piece of jewelry and glows with a pinkish hue that beguiles anyone who comes near it. While you are wearing this gem, any nondemon creature that ends its turn within 10 feet of you must succeed on a DC 14 Wisdom saving throw or become [[RPG Manager/Conditions/Obsessed]] with you until the start of its next turn. If the creature succeeds, it is immune to this item's effects for 1 hour.
