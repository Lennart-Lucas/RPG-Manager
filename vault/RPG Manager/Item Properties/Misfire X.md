A weapon starts with a misfire rating of 1. Each time you make an attack with the weapon, after adding your hit modifiers, compare the rolls result to the misfire rating. If the result is equal or lower than the current misfire score it misfires.
When a weapon misfires instead of making the attack it breaks, and cannot be used again until you spend an action to try and repair it. To repair the weapon, you must make a successful Tinker's Tools check (DC equal to the misfire rating). If your check fails, the weapon is broken and must be repaired out of combat at half the cost of the weapon.
