This condition brings a creature to the brink of falling asleep. Creatures immune to effects that would put them to sleep are immune to this condition. While a creature is drowsy, it is under the following effects:
- It moves at half speed.
- It has disadvantage on attack rolls, ability checks, and Dexterity saves.
- It can't concentrate.
- Attacks against it have advantage.
- The only spells it can cast are cantrips.
