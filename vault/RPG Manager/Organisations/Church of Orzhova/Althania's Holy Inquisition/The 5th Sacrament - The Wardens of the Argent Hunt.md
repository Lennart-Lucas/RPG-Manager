> *The Wardens of the Argent Hunt are set against that which is not of natural creation. Beasts, demons, and aberrations of corrupted flesh or will fall within their charge. Where doctrine ends and survival begins, they are granted sanction to pursue, to endure, and to eradicate.*

In 563 AR something came out of the [[Dornwald]], and over the eight winters that followed it emptied forty-one villages in the eastern marches. Levies were sent against it and did not come back. In the seventh year of that **Season of Teeth**, the Church raised the Fifth Sacrament out of whatever was left to hand: foresters, poachers, houndsmen, two apostate physicians, and one man who had survived three encounters and would never say how.

They heal the division of flesh. Creation is one and whole as it was made, and whatever is stitched, doubled or assembled out of parts is not creation and has no claim on mercy.

## What They Are
Wardens are not blessed, not chosen, and no stronger than other men. They are trained for eleven years, and the training is the whole of the order's secret: anatomy, toxicology, tracking, ritual, folk-lore, restraint under terror, and an enormous rote memory of what kills what.

A Warden does not fight a thing fairly. He learns it, poisons it, traps it, exhausts it, burns its ground, and kills it while it is looking somewhere else. Wardens who prefer to fight fairly make up a small portion of the order, and none of them are old.

They work alone or in pairs, riding a circuit of the marches, taking the Church's silver and the village's food, and they are welcome nowhere twice.

## Mandate
Their charge covers beasts and vermin of unnatural kind, things bred or warped or stitched; demons and possessions and whatever walks in from where it should not; and curses, hauntings, blighted ground and the burial of what will not stay buried. To do it they hold sanction to enter any land, break any door, dig any grave and burn any building, without a bishop's leave and without compensation to anyone.

They are explicitly not charged with cults, witches or covens, because human heresy belongs to [[The 6th Sacrament - The Keepers of the Last Flame|the Sixth]]. That division held for the first two centuries of the Fifth's existence. It has not held since the Edict of Cold Hearths turned the Sixth outward.

## Ranks
**Runners** are apprentices, through years one to eleven. Perhaps a third of them finish, and a tenth of those survive a first circuit. **Wardens** hold the field, and there are some four hundred of them in a realm of four million people. **Houndmasters** keep and breed the argent hounds at the chapter-houses and are the order's true institutional memory. **Masters of the Kill** are regional heads, each holding one circuit and one chapter-house.

At the head stands the Master of the Hunt, styled *the Silver Horn*. The seat is held by **Ottilie Reyn**, fifty-nine, formerly Master of the Kill on the Knarrholt circuit, who has petitioned the Conclave for more coin nine years running and been refused nine times.

## Mark and Habit
The order's colours are argent on green-black and its mark is a hunting horn crossed with a broken fang. Wardens otherwise dress like nothing in particular. The habit is a single silver-inlaid bracer worn on the left forearm, and every Warden's kit beyond that is his own, assembled over a career, so that no two are alike.

The **argent hounds** are bred for a nose that finds what does not smell like living meat. They are ugly, they are silent, and each of them is worth more than the man holding the leash.

## Standing
The Fifth is chronically under-funded and chronically over-committed, and it is the only Sacrament the common people are actually glad to see. A village will hide a Steward's cart and feed a Warden out of the same larder.

Its quarrel with the Sixth is the great open wound of the Conclave. The Sixth's turn outward has left domestic cults to sprout in the emptied villages, and by long custom no Warden may open a case against people. So the Wardens have taken to arriving late, entering a coven in the filing as a *beast-haunt*, and dealing with it anyway. That is a lie in a Church document, which makes it a matter for [[The 4th Sacrament - The Arbiters of Judgment|the Fourth]], and every person involved understands as much.

## As Matters Stand (958 AR)
The [[Knarrholt|Creakwood]] circuit on the Blackwood fringe has lost four Wardens in two years, four times its normal rate, and Reyn has moved her own household west to sit on the problem. The Dornwald has been formally unentered since 953: no writ will be issued for it, no Warden may go in, and the order will not say who took the decision.

In the emptied villages of Veldenau the Wardens keep finding shrines raised to nothing they have a name for. They file them as beast-haunts and they go home. Among the Runners it is said that Reyn has a standing instruction that any Warden who files a coven honestly is transferred to the eastern marches inside the month, and that the transfer is meant as protection rather than punishment.
