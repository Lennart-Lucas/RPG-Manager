> *The Shepherds of the Host are appointed to the souls of the armed faithful. Within armies and warbands they walk unseen, guiding the will of steel toward righteous obedience. They ensure that no blade is raised without divine sanction, and no commander's ambition exceeds the dominion of the Church.*

In 134 AR **Marshal Hedric Kaal** won the realm's northern war, and three years later he brought eleven thousand veterans to the gates of [[RPG Manager/Locations/Valmoria/Althania/Lucerna]] and asked, politely, to be invested with the lands he had taken. He was refused. Shortly afterwards his own captains killed him, and the Church has never wished to discover whether that would work a second time. The Second Sacrament was founded in 137 AR.

They heal the division of the sword. There is one army in Althania, and it does not belong to the Marshal.

## Mandate

A Shepherd is attached to every formation of two hundred men or more, whether it is a levy, a garrison, a sworn band or a bishop's own household troops. He does not command it. He **sanctions** it, and no march, siege, assault, sack, execution or truce is lawful without his word. That word is given fresh at every dawn.

Alongside the sanction he does the ordinary work of a chaplain. He hears confession, buries the dead, reads the rite before the line advances, and knows every man in the company by name and by sin. He also files, and his files go to Lucerna rather than to the general.

One further duty appears in no published Rule. Every soldier who has fought beside [[RPG Manager/Organisations/The Black Legion]] takes an oath administered by a Shepherd, and breaking it is among the four crimes for which the Fourth still uses the older sentence. Shepherds who have administered that oath do not discuss having done so, and the order has never explained why the task went to chaplains rather than to lawyers.

## The Two Faces

To the ranks, the Shepherd is the only officer who will sit in the mud with them. He writes their letters home, he has memorised which of them has a child and which has a debt, and he will absolve them for what they did in the village. Soldiers love their Shepherd. Nothing about that is accidental; it is doctrine, and it is trained.

It is also the mechanism. A Shepherd who **withdraws sanction** has no need to arrest a general. He steps into the yard, states that the campaign is no longer holy, and the man's own sergeants take him. The order teaches that this second face should be shown perhaps twice in a career, and that a Shepherd who needs it more often has already failed at the first.

Twice since the founding has a company refused its Shepherd. On both occasions [[RPG Manager/Organisations/Church of Orzhova/Althania's Holy Inquisition/The 4th Sacrament - The Arbiters of Judgment|the Fourth Sacrament]] came, and on both occasions the company ceased to exist as a legal entity. Its colours were effaced by the First, its pay was struck by the Third, and its survivors became landless men with no name to give.

## Ranks

Field Shepherds serve one to a company and are universally called **Crooks**, a name the soldiers gave them and the order has quietly adopted. **Shepherds of the Standard** are attached to a whole levy or campaign army. **The Fold** are the order's own enforcers, two dozen men who go and finish what a Crook could not; they are quiet, they are not chaplains, and they arrive together.

The head of the order is the High Shepherd, styled *Crook of the Host*. The seat is held by **Ulbrecht Sarne**, who was a Crook at the retreat from Kaltenmark and came home with fourteen men out of a company of three hundred. He does not speak about it, and he has never needed to.

## Mark and Habit

The order wears grey, corded in crimson, and its mark is a shepherd's crook crossed with a bared blade. In the field a Shepherd wears no armour over the breast whatever the fashion of the war. The custom is a doctrinal vanity and it kills roughly one Shepherd a year, which the order considers a reasonable price for what it buys in the ranks.

## Standing

The Second is the reason the Althanian nobility has never successfully rebelled. It is also, more and more, the reason Althania loses wars. Every sanction is a delay, and a Shepherd who is unsure will let a good opportunity die rather than risk an unholy one.

Relations with [[RPG Manager/Organisations/Church of Orzhova/Althania's Holy Inquisition/The 3rd Sacrament - The Stewards of Providence|the Third]] have gone from cold to open in the last two years, since the Stewards began auditing the commissariat of the Penitent Campaign and established that the army had been fed, on paper, twice.

## As Matters Stand (958 AR)

Five years on from Kaltenmark the Host is smaller, poorer, and full of men who were told the campaign was holy right up to the morning it was called off. Sarne has quietly stopped filing certain classes of report, and the Curia has quietly stopped asking for them.

The barracks tell a harder story, and the Second has never denied it, which the barracks find significant in itself. They say the Penitent Campaign lost sanction three weeks before the retreat began. They say the Shepherds went on reading the dawn rite regardless, because withdrawing it in the field would have destroyed the army faster than the enemy managed to. Among the older Crooks there is a version that goes further still, and holds that Sarne has not truly given sanction to anything since, and that every rite he has read in five years has been a formula that means nothing at all.
