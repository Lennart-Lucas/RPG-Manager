The Watchers of the Threshold discern that which lies beyond mortal certainty. Through vision, omen, and trial of the miraculous, they judge the unseen currents of fate and faith. It is by their discernment alone that true saints are recognized, false miracles condemned, and approaching calamity revealed.

The Third is the smallest Sacrament and the one whose work most directly governs the others. The Realm cannot afford to be everywhere: there are fewer than four hundred [[RPG Manager/Organisations/Althania's Holy Inquisition/5th Sacrament – The Wardens of the Argent Hunt|Wardens]] and a smaller number of Custodians on placement, against a continent. Somebody must decide where the scarce metal goes. That somebody is a room of sixty priests laying out cards.

# The Drawing
The order's instrument is the **Sortilege**: sixty painted cards, unchanged in composition since 214 AR, drawn in standing session at Weissturm by paired Watchers who work in silence and are not told what question they are answering. The reading is transcribed, sealed, and sent to the **Concordance**, where it is set against the day's petitions, dispatches, and parish returns until a correspondence is found. The output is an **Indication**: a numbered instruction naming a place, a season, and a kind of trouble.

Indications are not advice. A Warden warrant is issued against an Indication; a Custodian placement is opened against an Indication; a prince-bishop who ignores one and is subsequently proved right has still ignored one. Roughly four hundred issue a year. The Third's own review holds that a little better than half prove sound, perhaps a fifth are unfalsifiable, and the remainder are simply wrong — an accuracy the order publishes annually, defends as extraordinary, and is mocked for at every Sacred Diet by men who have no alternative to offer.

The Watchers themselves are careful in a way their reputation is not. The Third's foundational text holds that the cards show what is coming, never what should be done about it, and that the second question is a matter for men. Grand Watchers have resigned over the distinction. The current Grand Watcher, Sibylle Rauth, was elevated in 1172 AR on the strength of an Indication she had personally refused to sign.

# The Cause
The order's second charge is the **Cause**: the process by which a miracle is tried and a saint recognised. Any claim of the miraculous within the Realm — a healing, an incorrupt body, a weeping icon, a voice in a field — must be reported to the nearest Watcher chapter within forty days. Most are dismissed by a single priest in an afternoon. What survives goes to a full Cause, which is an adversarial proceeding: the postulant's case is argued by an advocate, and against it stands the **Contrary Voice**, a Watcher appointed to prove the thing false by any means short of fraud.

The Contrary Voice wins most of the time, and is meant to. In seven centuries the Realm has recognised one hundred and forty-one saints and condemned rather more than nine thousand claims, several hundred of them with prosecutions attached. Country clergy accordingly report miracles the way they report a fire — promptly, and with a certain dread, since a Cause that finds fraud finds it against somebody, and the somebody is usually local.

# Thresholds
The order's name is older than its divinatory work and refers to its third and least public charge: what comes *through*. A threshold, in the Third's usage, is any place where the world is thin — a barrow that should not be open, a well that answers, a door in a hillside that is not always there. The Watchers do not close thresholds so much as keep the ledger of them, and hand the consequences to whoever is chartered to deal with consequences.

The standing arrangement with the Fifth is that whatever comes through a threshold is the Third's problem and whatever has already settled in is the Fifth's. Everyone involved regards this line as unworkable, and the correspondence between the two orders on the subject of **Vestiges** runs to some sixty volumes in the [[RPG Manager/Organisations/Althania's Holy Inquisition/1st Sacrament – The Keepers of Doctrine|Sanctum]], most of it bad-tempered.

Ranks ascend: **Watchling** (novice; may transcribe but not draw), **Watcher**, **Watcher of the Concordance** (competent to issue an Indication), **Warden of the Ledger** (chapter command; keeps the threshold registry for a region), **Grand Watcher**. Watchers who draw are retired from drawing at fifty by rule, for reasons the order states plainly in its constitutions and has never elaborated on.
