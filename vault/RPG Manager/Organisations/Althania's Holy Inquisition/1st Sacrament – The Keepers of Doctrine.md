Charged are the Keepers of Doctrine with the preservation, purification, and definition of sacred truth. They are the voice by which scripture is made immutable and the silence by which forbidden knowledge is undone. No text, record, or utterance stands beyond their scrutiny, and what they declare absent from doctrine is as though it had never been.

The First is the oldest, largest, and least feared of the Sacraments, and by a considerable margin the most powerful. It commands no soldiers, hunts nothing, and has not executed a man in four hundred years. What it does instead is decide what is true, and having decided, arrange that nothing survives to contradict it. A prince-bishop who quarrels with the [[RPG Manager/Organisations/Althania's Holy Inquisition/6th Sacrament - The Custodians of the Black Pyre|Sixth]] may be taken in the night. A prince-bishop who quarrels with the First finds, some years later, that the argument he made is no longer in any book, that the precedent he relied on is marked doubtful in every copy of the register, and that clerks half his age do not understand what he is complaining about.

# The Definition
Doctrine is fixed by **Definition**: a ruling of the Keepers on a disputed point, issued under the Grand Keeper's seal and thereafter immutable. Definitions are rare — fewer than nine hundred in seven centuries — and are argued for decades before issue, because the order holds that a Definition cannot be revised, only clarified, and clarification of a clarification is regarded within the First as a professional humiliation.

Between Definitions sits the far larger business of **Referral**. Any court, chapter, abbey, or Sacrament may refer a question upward, and most do, since a ruling obtained from the Keepers is a shield nothing in the Realm can pierce. The order is correspondingly and deliberately slow. The [[RPG Manager/Organisations/Althania's Holy Inquisition/5th Sacrament – The Wardens of the Argent Hunt|Wardens]] twice referred the question of Norcaine moonsilver and were twice told the matter was under consideration, which every party understood to be the ruling. When the First declines to speak, the practice complained of continues, and the complaint is exhausted. This is not a failure of the mechanism. It is most of what the mechanism is for.

# The Sanctum
The order's archive at Weissturm is called the **Sanctum**, and it holds the Realm's memory: scripture in every recension, the acts of every Sacred Diet, seven centuries of [[RPG Manager/Organisations/Althania's Holy Inquisition/5th Sacrament – The Wardens of the Argent Hunt|Warden]] accountings in copy, the heresy files surrendered by the Sixth at the [[RPG Manager/Events/Reform of the Sixth|Reform]], and the confiscated libraries of every coven, cult, and condemned scholar the Inquisition has ever broken. It is headed by the **Archivist of the Sanctum**, an office held since 1162 AR by Hedwig Alscher, and it is the only body in the Realm entitled to possess what it forbids.

Against the Sanctum stands **Expurgation**, which is the order's other face. Where a text is condemned, the Keepers do not merely ban it; they recall it, and the works that cite it, and the works that cite those, correcting each by hand, and enter the condemned work in the **Register of Absent Things** — a catalogue, held in the Sanctum and read by perhaps forty people, of everything the Realm has been made to forget. The Register exists because the order discovered early that a forgetting nobody records is a forgetting that cannot be audited, and the Keepers audit everything.

That the First keeps a perfect account of its own erasures, and shows it to no one, is the standing objection of the [[RPG Manager/Organisations/Althania's Holy Inquisition/4th Sacrament – The Arbiters of Judgment|Arbiters of Judgment]], who have asked for access nine times and been referred, each time, to a Definition of 611 AR holding that the Sanctum is scripture's own house and not subject to inspection.

# Relations
The First is the Sacrament every other Sacrament needs and none of them like. It receives the Wardens' findings and turns them into what the Realm knows about its own monsters. It received the Sixth's domestic files in 691 AR and has never returned them, which the Sixth mentions at intervals. It ruled in 1174 AR that an allegation producing no verdict was no bar to episcopal election, and so seated **Father Edward** at [[RPG Manager/Locations/Valmoria/Althania/Salvamont|Salvamont]] over the Sixth's objection — a ruling the Keepers insist was narrow, technical, and about procedure, and which nobody in the Realm has ever read that way.

Ranks ascend: **Lector** (a novitiate of six years, entirely in reading), **Scribe Ordinal**, **Sacrist Ordinal** (competent to draft a Definition and to authorise an expurgation), **Prior of the Sanctum** (department command), **Grand Keeper**. The middle two ranks are older than the order, having been carried over whole from the Weissturm cathedral chapter that the First absorbed at the founding — which is the source of a discomfort the order has never quite put down. **Veit Adramar** was a Sacrist Ordinal of that chapter, and used the rank, the training, and the reading habits the First inherited from it to argue at the founding convocation that reading about covens is not the same as reaching them. He was given the [[RPG Manager/Organisations/Althania's Holy Inquisition/6th Sacrament - The Custodians of the Black Pyre|Sixth Sacrament]] to prove it. The First's own scholarly tradition thereby produced, in a single afternoon and over the objection of everyone who would go on to staff it, the one order in the Realm that answers to nobody the First can reach.
