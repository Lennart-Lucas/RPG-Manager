> *In the beginning there was One, and the One was whole.*
> *Then came the knives, and the knives were called gods.*
> — The Concordance, Book of the Voice, i.1

The Church of Orzhova is the sole lawful faith of [[RPG Manager/Locations/Valmoria/Althania]] and, in every sense that matters, its government. It has stood since the Rising and has never once admitted to changing. Its seat is [[RPG Manager/Locations/Valmoria/Althania/Lucerna]], and its writ reaches every parish register, every oath sworn, and every hearth in the realm.

## The One and the Undivided

Orzhova is not a name. The Keepers hold it to be a word of the older speech meaning *that which cannot be cut* — the Undivided, the Whole, the One That Remains. To give the One a name would be to set it beside other names, and there are no other names to set it beside.

The doctrine turns on a single idea, and everything in Althanian life descends from it. **Division is the wound.** The world was whole and was cut apart, the Nine Names were the knives that cut it, and every division since has reopened the same wound: divided truth, divided law, divided worship, divided flesh. Salvation is therefore not forgiveness but reunion, and sin is not disobedience but **severance** — the making of two where there was one.

From that comes everything the faithful are taught to fear. A second gospel. A second court. A second purse. A second god. A beast that is many creatures in one body. A man who is one thing in the square and another in his cellar. It also comes to two lines that every Althanian child can recite before they can read:

> *One God, one truth, one law, one realm.*
> *That which is divided is not holy.*

## The Concordance

There is one book, and it is called the **Concordance**. It was sealed in 61 AR at the close of the War of Two Gospels, twenty-seven years in which the young faith fought itself over which words the First Voice had actually spoken. It has two halves that do not entirely agree, and the Church resolves that difficulty by declaring that they do.

The **Book of the Voice** holds the sayings of Ilsandra. It is short, furious, and largely concerned with bread, masters, and the uselessness of intermediaries, and it is the half that gets people burned when they quote it too enthusiastically. The **Book of the Hand** sets out the ordering of the world under Orzhova — the offices, the tithes, the oaths, and the wars that are permitted — and was written, or at least assembled, under the first Hierarch.

The Concordance is immutable. It is also, on occasion, corrected. Correcting scripture is the sole prerogative of [[RPG Manager/Organisations/Church of Orzhova/Althania's Holy Inquisition/The 1st Sacrament - The Keepers of Doctrine|the First Sacrament]], and when a passage is removed the doctrine is not that it was cut but that it was **never there**. The technical term for the act is an *effacement*. The vernacular term for the result is a *never-was*.

Copies are struck on **impression engines**, of which there are eleven in the realm, every one licensed, sealed and counted. An unlicensed press is not a crime against property. It is a crime against the indivisibility of truth, and it is tried as heresy.

## The Saints

The One does not answer. The saints do, or the One answers through them, which the Concordance insists is the same thing and which every parish priest quietly treats as different.

Saints are made rather than born. A bishop opens a cause, and the **Attestors** of the First Sacrament are sent to break it: they interrogate the witnesses separately, take the relic apart, attempt the miracle by mundane means, and exhume the body if there is one to exhume. Nineteen causes in twenty collapse under that treatment. The twentieth is written into the Canon, and the see that holds the bones grows rich. The great saints and their patronages are set out in [[RPG Manager/Lore/The Canon of Saints]].

## Grace

Grace is real, and this is not in dispute. Men and women who pray in the proper forms close wounds, break fevers and stand in fire, and the realm has watched them do it. What is in dispute, quietly and in the right rooms, is *what answers*.

The Church teaches that grace flows from the One through the ordained and the acclaimed, and that any power rising outside that channel is a counterfeit worn by something else. The difficulty is that the counterfeit and the true coin look identical, feel identical, and heal identically. Hedge-wives in the fen close wounds too. The Concordance's answer is that the deed does not sanctify the doer, and that the fen-wife's patient is healed into damnation.

Curates in village parishes have been known to send for the fen-wife regardless, and to say nothing about it afterwards.

## The Sanctioned Fire

Arcane magic is a separate question and receives a separate answer, one line long, taught to every novice on the first day:

> *Fire is not sin. An unattended fire is.*

Sorcery is held to be a property of creation rather than a gift of the One, no different in kind from fire, or flood, or the strength of a horse. Wielding it is no heresy. Wielding it **unwatched** is the whole of the offence, because a power that answers only to the man holding it is a second authority, and there are no second authorities.

Every caster in Althania is therefore found young, registered by their curate, and taken north to the **White Tower** at [[RPG Manager/Locations/Valmoria/Althania/Weissturm]] to be trained, sealed and entered on the Roll of Sanctioned Grace. Most never leave it again. A caster off the Roll is **unwritten**, and hunting the unwritten belongs to [[RPG Manager/Organisations/Church of Orzhova/Althania's Holy Inquisition/The 6th Sacrament - The Keepers of the Last Flame|the Sixth Sacrament]].

## The Hierarchy

| Office | Charge |
| --- | --- |
| **The Hierarch** | Vicar Undivided; elected for life; the single point at which every authority in Althania meets. Presently Hierarch Vasmund III, at Lucerna. |
| **The Seven Elector-Bishops** | Prince-bishops of the seven great sees. They rule their lands as temporal lords and elect the Hierarch. |
| **The Curia** | The Hierarch's household and offices at Lucerna — chancery, seals, archives, protocol. Enormous, slow, and impossible to route around. |
| **Bishops** | One-and-thirty lesser sees. Some are wealthy. Most are not, and know it. |
| **Abbots and Prioresses** | Rule abbey-lands owing nothing to any bishop, only to the Hierarch. A perpetual grievance. |
| **Curates** | Parish priests. Underfed, half-lettered, and the only face of the Church nine in ten Althanians will ever see. |
| **The Sixfold Holy Inquisition** | Outside all of it. Answers to the Hierarch alone. See [[RPG Manager/Organisations/Church of Orzhova/Althania's Holy Inquisition]]. |

## The Year of the Church

The calendar begins at the Rising and turns on five great feasts.

- **The Rising** (first day of the year) — bells from midnight to midnight. Bread is given away. Debts are not forgiven, whatever the sermons imply.
- **The Ninefold Denial** (ninth day) — the old gods' names are spoken aloud, once, in order, by children, and denied. It is the only occasion on which speaking them is lawful, and it is the reason every Althanian peasant knows all nine. The First Sacrament has petitioned four times to have the rite reformed and has been refused four times.
- **The Ascension of the Voice** (high summer) — the taking-up of Ilsandra at [[RPG Manager/Locations/Valmoria/Althania/Salvamont]], and the great pilgrimage of the realm.
- **The Sealing** — the closing of the Concordance. Scripture is read aloud without pause for a day and a night, in Ecclesiast, which almost nobody in the congregation understands.
- **Cold Hearths** (deep winter) — instituted in 841 AR to mark the quenching of the public pyres. Fires are put out at dusk and relit at dawn from a single flame carried from the church. Households that fail to relight are counted, and the count goes to the Sixth.

## The Faith That Was

Before the Rising the realm served the **Nine Names** — Aonir of the star-field first among them, and Nor, and Elen, Ereon, Zarach, Ulm, Hirin, Niethalf and Shanna. Their priesthoods were drawn from the Crowned Houses, so that a man's lord and a man's god were cousins, and they took a fifth of everything.

The Keepers record nine. The Keepers also record that there were never more than nine, which is the sort of sentence that tells a careful reader a great deal.

The old faith is dead. It also survives in every harvest-knot hung over a byre door, every oak left uncut at the corner of a field, and every coin pressed into a spring. The Church's official position is that these are superstitions emptied of meaning. The Sixth Sacrament's position, which appears only in its own ledgers, is that a superstition is a rite which has forgotten that it is one, and that rites remember.

## As Matters Stand (958 AR)

Hierarch Vasmund III is dying, and the Church is discovering how much of its machinery assumed he would not. The Curia issues his decrees over a signature the copyists no longer trust. The Elector-Bishops have begun spending against a conclave nobody will name aloud.

Beneath that, the older quarrel is stirring again. The Book of the Voice has always been the difficult half of the Concordance, and a rumour has been running through the Scribes' Quarter at Lucerna since last autumn that a warrant was drawn against a portion of it. The First Sacrament has not confirmed the rumour. It has also not done the thing it normally does with a false one, which is to efface it.
