# The Church of Orzhova

> *In the beginning there was One, and the One was whole.*
> *Then came the knives, and the knives were called gods.*
> — The Concordance, Book of the Voice, i.1

The Church of Orzhova is the sole lawful faith of [[RPG Manager/Locations/Valmoria/Althania]] and, in every practical sense, its government. It has been here since the Rising, and it has never once admitted to changing.

Its seat is [[RPG Manager/Locations/Valmoria/Althania/Lucerna]].

## The One and the Undivided

Orzhova is not a name. The Keepers hold it to be a word of the older speech meaning *that which cannot be cut* — the Undivided, the Whole, the One That Remains. To give the One a name would be to set it beside other names, and there are no other names.

The doctrine turns on a single idea, from which everything else in Althanian life descends:

**Division is the wound.** The world was whole and was cut apart. The Nine Names were the knives — fragments, usurpers, or liars, depending on which Article one reads — and every division since is the same wound reopened: divided truth, divided law, divided worship, divided flesh. Salvation is not forgiveness. Salvation is *reunion*. Sin is not disobedience but **severance**: to make two where there was one.

From this comes everything the faithful are taught to fear. A second gospel. A second court. A second purse. A second god. A beast that is many creatures in one body. A man who is one thing in the square and another in his cellar.

It also comes to this, which every Althanian child can recite before they can read:

> *One God, one truth, one law, one realm.*
> *That which is divided is not holy.*

## The Concordance

There is one book. It is called the **Concordance**, and it was sealed in 61 AR at the close of the War of Two Gospels — twenty-seven years in which the young faith fought itself over which words the First Voice had actually said. It has two halves that do not entirely agree, a fact the Church resolves by declaring that they do.

- **The Book of the Voice** — the sayings of Ilsandra, the First Voice. Short, furious, largely concerned with bread, masters, and the uselessness of intermediaries. It is the half that gets people burned when quoted too enthusiastically.
- **The Book of the Hand** — the ordering of the world under Orzhova: the offices, the tithes, the oaths, the wars that are permitted. Written, or at least assembled, under the first Hierarch.

The Concordance is immutable. It is also, on occasion, corrected. The correction of scripture is the sole prerogative of [[RPG Manager/Organisations/Church of Orzhova/Althania's Holy Inquisition/The 1st Sacrament - The Keepers of Doctrine]], and when a passage is removed, the doctrine is not that it was cut — the doctrine is that it was **never there**. The technical term is an *effacement*; the vernacular term is a *never-was*.

Copies are struck on **impression engines**, of which there are eleven in the realm, all licensed, sealed and counted. An unlicensed press is not a crime against property. It is a crime against the indivisibility of truth, and it is tried as heresy.

## The Saints

The One does not answer. The saints do — or the One answers through them, which the Concordance insists is the same thing and every parish priest quietly treats as different.

Saints are made, not born. A cause is opened by a bishop, and the **Attestors** of the First Sacrament are sent: they interrogate witnesses separately, take the relic apart, try to reproduce the miracle by mundane means, and exhume the body if there is one. Roughly nineteen causes in twenty collapse. The twentieth is written into the Canon, and the see that holds the bones becomes rich.

The great saints and their patronages are set out in [[RPG Manager/Lore/The Canon of Saints]].

## Grace

Grace is real. This is not in dispute; men and women who pray in the proper forms close wounds, break fevers, and stand in fire. What is in dispute — quietly, in the right rooms — is *what answers*.

The Church teaches that grace flows from the One through the ordained and the acclaimed, and that any power arising outside that channel is a counterfeit worn by something else. The difficulty is that the counterfeit and the true coin look identical, feel identical, and heal identically. Hedge-wives in the fen close wounds too. The Concordance's answer is that the deed does not sanctify the doer, and that the fen-wife's patient is healed into damnation.

Priests of village parishes have been known to send for the fen-wife anyway, and to say nothing about it afterwards.

## The Sanctioned Fire

Arcane magic is a separate question and gets a separate answer, which is one line long and is taught to every novice on the first day:

> *Fire is not sin. An unattended fire is.*

Sorcery is held to be a property of creation rather than a gift of the One — like fire, or flood, or the strength of a horse. Wielding it is no heresy. Wielding it **unwatched** is the whole of the offence, because a power answering only to the man who holds it is a second authority, and there are no second authorities.

So every caster is found young, registered by their curate, and taken north to the **White Tower** at [[RPG Manager/Locations/Valmoria/Althania/Weissturm]] to be trained, sealed, and entered on the Roll of Sanctioned Grace. Most never leave it. A caster off the Roll is **unwritten**, and hunting the unwritten belongs to [[RPG Manager/Organisations/Church of Orzhova/Althania's Holy Inquisition/The 6th Sacrament - The Keepers of the Last Flame]].

## The Hierarchy

| Office | Charge |
| --- | --- |
| **The Hierarch** | Vicar Undivided; elected for life; the single point at which every authority in Althania meets. Presently Hierarch Vasmund III, at Lucerna. |
| **The Seven Elector-Bishops** | Prince-bishops of the seven great sees. They rule their lands as temporal lords and elect the Hierarch. |
| **The Curia** | The Hierarch's household and offices at Lucerna — chancery, seals, archives, protocol. Enormous, slow, and impossible to route around. |
| **Bishops** | One-and-thirty lesser sees. Some are wealthy. Most are not, and know it. |
| **Abbots and Prioresses** | Rule abbey-lands owing nothing to any bishop, only to the Hierarch. A perpetual grievance. |
| **Curates** | Parish priests. Underfed, half-lettered, and the only face of the Church nine in ten Althanians will ever see. |
| **The Sixfold Holy Inquisition** | Outside all of it. Answers to the Hierarch alone. See [[RPG Manager/Organisations/Church of Orzhova/Althania's Holy Inquisition]]. |

## The Year of the Church

The calendar begins at the Rising and turns on five great feasts.

- **The Rising** (first day of the year) — bells from midnight to midnight. Bread is given away. Debts are not forgiven, whatever the sermons imply.
- **The Ninefold Denial** (ninth day) — the old gods' names are spoken aloud, once, in order, by children, and denied. It is the only occasion on which speaking them is lawful. It is also the reason every Althanian peasant knows all nine, which the First Sacrament has petitioned four times to change and been refused four times.
- **The Ascension of the Voice** (high summer) — the taking-up of Ilsandra at [[RPG Manager/Locations/Valmoria/Althania/Salvamont]]. The great pilgrimage.
- **The Sealing** — the closing of the Concordance. Scripture read aloud without pause for a day and a night, in Ecclesiast, which almost nobody understands.
- **Cold Hearths** (deep winter) — instituted 841 AR to mark the quenching of the public pyres. Fires are put out at dusk and relit at dawn from a single flame carried from the church. Households that cannot relight are counted, and the list goes to the Sixth.

## The Faith That Was

Before the Rising there were the **Nine Names** — Aonir of the star-field first among them, and Nor, and Elen, Ereon, Zarach, Ulm, Hirin, Niethalf, Shanna — served by priesthoods drawn from the Crowned Houses, so that a man's lord and a man's god were cousins.

The Keepers record nine. The Keepers also record that there were never more than nine, which is the sort of sentence that tells you a great deal.

The old faith is dead. It is also in every harvest-knot hung over a byre door, every oak left uncut at the corner of a field, every coin pressed into a spring. The Church's official position is that these are superstitions emptied of meaning. The Sixth Sacrament's position, expressed only in its own ledgers, is that a superstition is a rite that has forgotten it is one, and that rites remember.

## Errors Commonly Repeated

*Recorded here for correction, as the Canon requires.*

- That the First Voice did not ascend at Salvamont but was silenced there, by those who stood nearest her.
- That the Book of the Voice as sealed is a third of what was spoken, and that the missing portion concerned priests.
- That the impression engines exist to make scripture uniform, not plentiful.
- That the Ninefold Denial was written by a man who wanted the names remembered.
- That the One does not answer, has never answered, and that what answers has been very patient.
