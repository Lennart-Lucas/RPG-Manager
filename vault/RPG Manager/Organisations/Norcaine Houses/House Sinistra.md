## Description

Weapons and black magic used together. The darkblades and death knights, whose armour trembles with the power worked into it, and who fight and cast as a single discipline rather than two. Where death is Drach'on's craft and Archon's science, Sinistra treats it as worship. The house is smaller than either and considerably more dangerous per head.
