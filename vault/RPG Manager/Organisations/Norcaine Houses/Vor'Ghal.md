## Description

The deep. Tunnel-wrights, siege-miners, and the conjurors of the great Silver Halls beneath the mountains, who call the Arachnis out of their cold hideouts and into war. Vor'Ghal is the oldest house and the smallest, keeps its own calendar, marries almost exclusively within itself, and is regarded by the rest of Shall as something between a national treasure and a problem that has not yet happened
