## Description

Steel and discipline. The martial tradition: the assassins of the grey schools, the battlemasters, the strategists who give Norcaine armies their manoeuvre. Drach'on holds that an honourable life is one spent fighting the Light, keeps the oldest and cruellest training establishments in Shall, and regards every other house as a merchant of some kind.
