Black magic and governance. The towers, the academies, the necromantic schools from which the screams of the servants echo day and night. Archon produces the realm's warlocks and most of its administrators, and is the house most likely to win an argument it did not enter. Drach'on considers it a house of faithless careerists, which Archon does not dispute so much as fail to understand as an insult.
