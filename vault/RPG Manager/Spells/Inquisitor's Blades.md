You summon three swords of celestial steel that hover in your space, and which follow you for the spell's duration. When you cast the spell, and as a Bonus Action on each of your turns thereafter, you can launch one of the swords at a creature within 120 feet, making a ranged spell attack against the target. On a hit, the target takes 2d6 Slashing damage and 1d6 [[RPG Manager/Damage Types/Radiant]] damage. Fiends take an additional 1d6 [[RPG Manager/Damage Types/Radiant]] damage. Whether they hit or miss, the swords vanish into light after being launched.

## At higher levels

You summon one additional sword for each spell slot level above 2.
