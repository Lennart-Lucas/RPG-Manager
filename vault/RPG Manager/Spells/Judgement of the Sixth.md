You condemn a creature you can see within range. A blackened stake bursts from the ground beneath it, spectral chains lashing it in place as kindling catches at its feet.
The target must make a Dexterity saving throw. On a failed save, it takes 4d6 [[RPG Manager/Damage Types/Fire]] damage and is [[RPG Manager/Conditions/Restrained]] by the chains. On a successful save, it takes half as much damage, isn't [[RPG Manager/Conditions/Restrained]], and the spell ends.
A creature [[RPG Manager/Conditions/Restrained]] this way takes 3d6 [[RPG Manager/Damage Types/Fire]] damage at the start of each of its turns. At the end of each of its turns, it can make a Strength or Dexterity saving throw (its choice), ending the spell on a success. The pyre sheds bright light in a 20-foot radius and dim light for 20 feet beyond.
**The Crowd Recoils.** While the pyre burns, it radiates dread out to 15 feet. Any creature of your choice other than the target that can see the burning creature must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw when the pyre ignites, when it first enters the area on a turn, or when it starts its turn there, or become [[RPG Manager/Conditions/Frightened]] until the end of its next turn. The pyre — not you — is the source of this fear. A creature that succeeds on this save is immune to the pyre's dread for 1 minute.

## At higher levels

When you cast this spell using a slot of 5th level or higher, you can condemn one additional creature for each slot level above 4th. The creatures must be within 30 feet of each other. A creature can be frightened by only one pyre at a time.
