## Description

For the duration, you connect to the hive mind so you can see through the eyes of any bee, wasp or hornet that is in the same plane than you. To do so, choose a place you know, and you then see through the eyes of the nearest member of the hive.
You can change your focus to see through the eyes of an other member of the hive as an action.
In addition, for the duration, all bees, wasps and hornets are charmed by you and consider you as a member of their hive.
