Aurelien Sarre held the see of [[RPG Manager/RPG Manager/Locations_Valmoria_Althania_Salvamont|Salvamont]] for twenty-two years, sat in the Electoral Synod that chooses the Hierarch, and is remembered throughout the Holy [[RPG Manager/RPG Manager/Locations_Valmoria_Althania|Althanian Realm]] as the first man the elven rising of 1173 AR killed. He was in fact its leader. He was born in the low quarter to an elven mother, took the cathedral's charity school at seven, and was never at any point in his life formally examined as to his blood.

# Before
Sarre's origins were an open secret of the incurious kind — he was pale, he was slight, he had come up from the river, and Salvamont had spent two centuries arranging not to look closely at things of that sort. He was ordained at twenty-four, kept the chapter's accounts for eleven years, and was elected to the see in 1151 AR by a chapter that valued him for being unobjectionable.

What survives of his episcopate is a paper trail nobody has ever reread. Between 1154 and 1169 AR he laid eleven petitions before the **Sacred Diet** concerning the separate register, the guild bar, and the low quarter's confinement by custom. All eleven are in the acts. All eleven were recorded and nothing followed from them, which is the standard fate of a petition at the Diet and was not, at the time, remarkable. He stopped petitioning in 1169 AR. He opened the gates four years later.

# The rising
The river gates of Salvamont were not stormed. They were opened from the inside, at night, on the prince-bishop's own word, and the garrison broke three days later because it had been disposed by a man who intended it to. For five weeks Sarre commanded a city in open revolt against the Realm while the surviving chapter of the Sixfold Inquisition sat besieged in the citadel and knew precisely who was commanding it.

He died on the eleventh day of the recapture, in harness, directing the defence of the cathedral square from the steps of his own cathedral, and was killed there by the [[RPG Manager/RPG Manager/Organisations_Band of Bob|Band of Bob]] — a free company in the host [[RPG Manager/RPG Manager/Organisations_Althania's Holy Inquisition_5th Sacrament – The Wardens of the Argent Hunt|Father Edward]] had gathered out of the parishes. He was armoured and unhelmed. The company that killed him did not learn whose face it was until afterward.

# The martyrdom
The account the Realm holds — that Sarre was cut down by the rising on his cathedral steps in its first days — was composed in the citadel during the siege, by men who had been driven out of that cathedral by him personally.

Their reasoning was not cowardice and was never really disguised as piety. If a half-elf had been unlawfully invested, then every ordination Sarre had performed in twenty-two years was in question, every Oath of Chains he had witnessed was in question, and his vote in the Electoral Synod was in question. The [[RPG Manager/RPG Manager/Organisations_Althania's Holy Inquisition_4th Sacrament – The Arbiters of Judgment|Arbiters]] had administered his own oath at investiture without examination; the [[RPG Manager/RPG Manager/Organisations_Althania's Holy Inquisition_3rd Sacrament – The Watchers of the Threshold|Watchers]] had never drawn him. There was no version of the truth that stopped at Salvamont.

So the chapter moved his death forward by six weeks and handed it to the rebels, and the only people outside the citadel who could have contradicted them were elven, or dead, or in Edward's host. Nothing was expurgated, because nothing had to be. The lie was never filed with the [[RPG Manager/RPG Manager/Organisations_Althania's Holy Inquisition_1st Sacrament – The Keepers of Doctrine|Keepers of Doctrine]] and never entered the Register of Absent Things; it was simply written down first, by the only witnesses anyone thought to ask.

Some months later that same chapter elected Father Edward, who knew better, and Edward accepted the seat, and has never once used the episcopal style.

# The counter-record
The elves of the low quarter know exactly who opened the gates for them. It is not a secret there and never has been; it is a thing said at table, and to children, and over the graves of the second month's reprisals. It has no documentary existence whatsoever, and the men who would need to believe it are the men least able to.

Sarre is accordingly two people. In the Realm he is a martyr, invoked at Salvamont's dedication feast, and there is a painted panel of his death in the north transept that gets the architecture right and everything else wrong. In the low quarter he is called *the Gatekeeper*, without affection by everyone — for what he did, and for the two hundred years he waited to do it.
