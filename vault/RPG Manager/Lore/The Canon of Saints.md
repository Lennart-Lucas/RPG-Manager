# The Canon of Saints

*Abridged from the Canon kept at Lucerna. Feast days as observed in the Weissturm use; local uses vary and are tolerated where they are not noticed.*

The One does not answer. The saints do. Whether that is the same thing is a settled question, and it is settled in the affirmative, and nobody with sense discusses it further.

A cause is opened by a bishop and proved — or, nineteen times in twenty, disproved — by the **Attestors** of [[The 1st Sacrament - The Keepers of Doctrine]]. Relics of a canonised saint make a see rich. This is why the Canon is short and the Attestors are unpopular.

---

## The First Voice

**Saint Ilsandra, the First Voice** — *Ilse of Endhoff.* Water-carrier of the silver pits; spoke the Word in the pit-yard in 1 AR; taken up at [[Salvamont]] in 10 AR in the presence of her seven Companions.

*Patron of:* the poor, the spoken word, water, and those who must say a thing that will cost them.
*Feast:* the Ascension of the Voice, high summer. The great pilgrimage of the realm.
*Sign:* an open hand, palm up, empty.

The Book of the Voice is hers. It is the shortest half of scripture, the angriest, and the one most often quoted by people shortly before something happens to them.

## The First Hierarch

**Saint Bertaldus the Hand** — *Bertald Grauhand*, pit-captain, war-leader of the Nine Years' Rising, first Hierarch, who felled the Whiteroot and raised [[Weissturm]] over its stump.

*Patron of:* soldiers, miners, oaths, and lawful authority.
*Feast:* the Sealing.
*Sign:* a grey hand closed upon a stone.

The Book of the Hand is his, or was assembled under him, which the Canon treats as identical.

## The Seven Companions

Those who stood at the Ascension. Each founded a see, and their bones are the reason those seven sees are the electoral seven.

| Saint | See | Patron of |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Saint Vigila of the Threshold** | Ternovum | Gatekeepers, tollmen, watchmen, honest crossings. Her sign is a doorway with no door. |
| **Saint Ottmar of the True Weight** | Aschenberg | Miners, smiths, weights and measures. Died refusing a bribe he could have taken without anyone knowing. |
| **Saint Perenne the Mute** | Lucerna | Scribes, archivists, the keeping of secrets. Cut out her own tongue rather than repeat a confession. Attestors pray to her, which is a joke the order does not make. |
| **Saint Halvard Nine-Wounds** | Hohenfurt | Soldiers of the marches, the last stand, the wounded who keep walking. |
| **Saint Amalthe of the Silver Chain** | Rabenmark | Prisoners, hostages, the ransomed, and those who must bargain. |
| **Saint Cordwen the Ploughman** | Veldenau | Fields, seed, famine, patience. The most-loved saint in the realm by an enormous margin, and the only one whose shrines have no gold in them. |
| **Saint Kunrad the Ash** | Salvamont | Fire, judgement, and the burial of the burned. His cult was small for seven hundred years and then, after 701 AR, was not. |

## Lesser Saints of Note

- **Saint Elvine of the Fen** — hedge-wives, midwives, fevers. Canonised in 402 AR after a cause the Attestors spent nine years trying to break. She is the patron of precisely the people [[The 6th Sacrament - The Keepers of the Last Flame]] was founded to burn, and the awkwardness of this is felt by everyone and stated by nobody. Her shrine is at [[Dunvicum]].
- **Saint Grimald of the Horn** — Wardens, hunters, hounds, and the killing of what should not exist. Not formally in the Canon; his cause has been open since 604 AR. [[The 5th Sacrament - The Wardens of the Argent Hunt]] venerate him anyway, and the First Sacrament has decided not to make an issue of it.
- **Saint Marit of the Coin** — almsgiving and honest debt. Popular with the Providence Houses, which have paid for eleven of her shrines.

## The Un-Canonised

Fourteen saints have been removed from the Canon since the Sealing. Their names are not recorded here, obviously.

Their feast days, however, are still kept in some villages — the day is observed, the bread is baked, and nobody present can quite say who it is for. The Keepers of Doctrine regard this as an administrative matter. The Keepers of the Last Flame regard it as an early symptom.

## Errors Commonly Repeated

- That the First Voice did not ascend but was silenced, and that the seven Companions are seven because there was an eighth.
- That Saint Cordwen's shrines hold no gold because his pilgrims are poor, rather than because they will not give it.
- That Saint Grimald's cause has stood open for three and a half centuries because closing it either way would require someone to write down what he actually hunted.
