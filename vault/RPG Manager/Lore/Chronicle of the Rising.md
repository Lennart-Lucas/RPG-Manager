*Being the reckoning of years from the Rising, as kept in the Great Register at Lucerna and abridged for parish use.*

Althania reckons its years as **AR**, *Anno Resurgentis*, the Year of the Rising, and the vernacular says simply *after the Rising*. The present year is **958 AR**. Dates before the Rising are given as *of the Diademed Age* and are not kept precisely, on the grounds that nothing in them requires remembering.

## The Diademed Age (before 1 AR)

[[RPG Manager/Locations/Valmoria/Althania]] was ruled by the **Crowned Houses**, whose sons held the land and whose daughters held the temples, so that a man's lord and a man's god were cousins. Nine gods were served, the **Nine Names**, with Aonir of the star-field first among them. Their priesthoods took a fifth of everything, which is twice the tithe, and the Church mentions it often.

**c. 412 years before the Rising — the War of Six Crowns ends.** Six peoples fought it across the world: humans, dwarves, dark elves, high elves, orcs and trolls. The Keepers hold the period thinly, as they hold all of the Diademed Age. The [[The Dark Elves|Norcaine]] call the war the **Winnowing**, withdrew from it into fortified city-states rather than lose it, and have counted their years from that withdrawal ever since. [[Shall]] was raised in its closing years.

## The Rising (1–9 AR)

**1 AR — The Tally Riot.** In a famine winter the mine-tithe was raised at **Endhoff**, beneath the Aschen range, and the pit-yard filled with angry diggers. A water-carrier named **Ilse**, later canonised as Saint Ilsandra, spoke to them there. The Book of the Voice records what she said: *"There is not one god for the lord and another for the ox. There is One, and it is not theirs."* The riot did not stop. It converted.

**1–9 AR — The Nine Years' Rising.** The pit-companies took the mines, then the valleys, then the temples, under the war-leadership of a pit-captain named **Bertald Grauhand**. The Crowned Houses took nine years to fall. Their priesthoods were killed to the last man in three.

**8 AR — The Burning at the Thornwood.** The last priests of the Nine Names were taken east and burned on the ground that is now the [[Dornwald]]. The Church records the Nine as ended in that fire. Across the World Spine, in [[Shall]], the temples of Nor did not close for a day, and have not closed since.

**9 AR — The Felling of the Whiteroot.** The white-barked oak of Aonir, oldest holy thing in Althania, was cut down and burned, its stump capped in stone, and a **White Tower** raised over the cap. The place is [[RPG Manager/Locations/Valmoria/Althania/Weissturm]], and the Hierarch is styled *Keeper of the Whiteroot Stone* to this day.

**10 AR — The Ascension of the Voice.** Ilsandra was taken up at Endhoff in the presence of her seven Companions. The Church keeps the day as the second feast of the year. The villages around [[RPG Manager/Locations/Valmoria/Althania/Salvamont]] keep it differently, and quietly.

**11 AR — The Concord of Ash.** The first Concord met, acclaimed Bertald Grauhand as Hierarch, proscribed the Nine Names, and declared the [[RPG Manager/Organisations/Church of Orzhova]] the sole and undivided faith of the realm.

**12 AR — Endhoff renamed Salvamont**, the Mount of Salvation. The locals went on saying Endhoff, and still do, and have made a joke of it that the Church has never managed to stamp out.

**30 AR — The Seat comes down from Endhoff.** The Hierarch's household moved to a new foundation on the empty plain where the Elve meets the Rill, raised deliberately on ground where no Name had ever been spoken. The city is [[RPG Manager/Locations/Valmoria/Althania/Lucerna]]. Its electoral vote was suppressed in the same instrument, for the obvious reason.

## The Sealing (34–61 AR)

**34–61 AR — The War of Two Gospels.** The Voice and the Hand had not said the same things, and their followers went to war over the difference. It ran twenty-seven years, and by its end more Orzhovans had died at Orzhovan hands than the Crowned Houses ever managed.

**61 AR — The Sealing of the Concordance.** Both gospels were bound into one book, declared to agree, and closed. [[RPG Manager/Organisations/Church of Orzhova/Althania's Holy Inquisition/The 1st Sacrament - The Keepers of Doctrine|The First Sacrament]] was founded in the same year to keep them agreeing.

## The Building of the Six

**134–137 AR — The March of the Marshal.** Marshal **Hedric Kaal** won the northern war and brought eleven thousand veterans to Lucerna to demand investiture. He was refused, and his own captains killed him. [[RPG Manager/Organisations/Church of Orzhova/Althania's Holy Inquisition/The 2nd Sacrament - The Shepherds of the Host|The Second Sacrament]] was founded in 137.

**214 AR — The Concord of Stone.** After long and undocumented unpleasantness at the mountain-gate, Althania and the thanes of the World Spine settled the question of Weissturm: the surface belongs to Althania, the rock to the Thane, the Gate is shared, and neither faith preaches to the other. In the same year the seventh crosier was raised at **Marisund**, replacing the vote Lucerna had given up.

**306 AR — The Bought Conclave.** A river-trade cartel purchased three electors and seated a Hierarch of its choosing. He reigned nine months, and four sees were insolvent when he died.

**311 AR — The Watchers of the Threshold** were founded as toll-wardens on the Tern crossings. The order was reformed and renamed [[RPG Manager/Organisations/Church of Orzhova/Althania's Holy Inquisition/The 3rd Sacrament - The Stewards of Providence|the Stewards of Providence]] in 344, when it became clear that the money had never crossed at the bridges.

**395 AR — The Edict of the Tower.** Every caster in the realm was ordered found, registered and gathered to the White Tower at Weissturm. The **Collegium of Sanctioned Grace** was instituted and the Roll opened, on a doctrine of one line: *fire is not sin; an unattended fire is.*

**415 AR — The Impunity.** The Elector-Bishop of Veldenau had two inquisitors killed in his own hall, and no court in Althania proved competent to try him.

**418 AR — [[RPG Manager/Organisations/Church of Orzhova/Althania's Holy Inquisition/The 4th Sacrament - The Arbiters of Judgment|The Fourth Sacrament]] founded.** Its first tribunal, the Judgment at Veldenau, hanged that bishop from his own bell-frame in his vestments.

**487–494 AR — The First Dark War.** Shall came down the Low Pass out of the World Spine. Salvamont was besieged for eleven months and held.

**491 AR — The Waking.** In the seventh month of the siege, miners cutting sideways from the Deep Workings in search of water broke into a sealed gallery older than the Diademed Age. What was inside it stood up. [[RPG Manager/Organisations/The Black Legion]] took the field the following spring, and the Lectors issued an opinion under seal which they have never republished.

**563–571 AR — The Season of Teeth.** Something came out of the Dornwald and emptied forty-one villages over eight winters. Two levies marched east and did not return.

**570 AR — [[RPG Manager/Organisations/Church of Orzhova/Althania's Holy Inquisition/The 5th Sacrament - The Wardens of the Argent Hunt|The Fifth Sacrament]] founded** out of foresters, poachers and survivors. The Season ended the following year, and the order has never claimed that it ended it.

**698 AR — The Grey Communion.** The diocese of Aschenberg was found to have been served for two generations by a coven wearing the offices and faces of its clergy. Every rite performed there since 649 was declared void, and forty thousand souls were declared unbaptised and baptised again.

**701 AR — [[RPG Manager/Organisations/Church of Orzhova/Althania's Holy Inquisition/The 6th Sacrament - The Keepers of the Last Flame|The Sixth Sacrament]] founded.**

## The Century of Pyres (701–838 AR)

The Sixth burned for a hundred and thirty-seven years and recorded forty-one thousand lawful pyres. Whole guilds were extinguished in the course of it, and three towns were struck from the register and appear on no map.

**766–773 AR — The Second Dark War.** Shall came down the Low Pass again and in greater strength, while the Sixth was still burning witches at home. Salvamont held a second time. The Black Legion lost nearly half its number and could not replace one of them.

**773 AR — The Truce of the Low Pass.** It was signed at the head of the pass, in the open, by parties who did not dismount. Nobody on either side has ever called it a peace, and the raiding has never entirely stopped.

**841 AR — The Edict of Cold Hearths.** The public burnings were quenched, officially because the fire had done its work. The Sixth was not disbanded. It went quiet, and it turned east.

## The Schism and the Wars (872–955 AR)

**872 AR — The Schism of the East.** The free cities beyond the Tern renounced the Hierarch while keeping the faith, and formed the **Sundered Communion**. Doctrinally this is the worst thing that has ever happened to Althania, because it is not unbelief but a second Church.

**874–901 AR — The Bitter Decades.** Three wars were fought in the Tern basin. All three were indecisive and all three were expensive.

**939 AR — The Last Keeper is named for the last time.** The head of the Sixth is entered in a Church register in that year and in no register since.

**944 AR — The Swift Conclave.** **Vasmund III**, Elector-Bishop of Salvamont, was elected in two days, the shortest conclave in four hundred years. The Third audited all seven electors beforehand, as it had done since 311, and found nothing, as it had done since 311.

**951–955 AR — The Penitent Campaign.** A war of recovery east of the Tern was preached as a pilgrimage and funded on Providence House paper. It ended in the **retreat from Kaltenmark**, where an army of nineteen thousand became something under seven. Two sees lost their reputations by it and five lost their solvency.

## The Present Age

**953 AR — The Dornwald is unentered.** The Fifth issues no writ for it, permits no Warden to enter, and offers no explanation to anyone.

**956–957 AR — The Coughing Winter.** Villages emptied across Veldenau and Rabenmark, and the landlords enclosed the vacancies. The countryside has been quiet ever since, in the particular way it was quiet in the last years of the Diademed Age.

**958 AR — Now.** Hierarch Vasmund III has not appeared at a public rite in eleven months, and his decrees issue in a hand that is not his. The Ash Font at Lucerna has been swept, checked, and swept again. Everyone is spending.
