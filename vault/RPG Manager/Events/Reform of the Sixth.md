The Diet of Caldreth was convened in 689 ostensibly to consider dissolving [[RPG Manager/Organisations/Althania's Holy Inquisition/6th Sacrament - The Custodians of the Black Pyre|the Sixth]]. Fourteen prince-bishops spoke for dissolution. It concluded instead that the Realm possessed in the order a capability it could not rebuild — a standing corps trained to live undetected inside hostile bodies for years — and that the only real question was where to point it. Grand Custodian Iselde von Marbach (r. 691–718 AR) carried out the reorganization. Domestic inquisitorial powers were suspended in practice though not revoked in law; the heresy files went to the [[RPG Manager/Organisations/Althania's Holy Inquisition/1st Sacrament – The Keepers of Doctrine|Keepers of Doctrine]]; and a new mandate directed the Sixth against "the covens of foreign princes." Her Upon the Ordering of the Sixth (694 AR) remains the order's doctrinal foundation.
Critics observed that the Reform changed the target and almost nothing else. Von Marbach agreed with them in writing: "We have not been made into something new. We have merely been given a border to stand behind." The Garden continued to be used throughout. It is used still.
