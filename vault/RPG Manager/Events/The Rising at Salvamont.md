[[RPG Manager/RPG Manager/Locations_Valmoria_Althania_Salvamont|Salvamont]] held the largest elven population of any city in the Realm, and held it badly: barred from the guilds, from the magistracy, and from ownership within the walls, taxed under a separate register, and confined by custom rather than statute to the low quarter along the river. The arrangement had been described as stable for two hundred years, which is a word Althanian administrators use for arrangements nobody has counted the cost of.

# The received account
The rising took the river gates in a night, broke the garrison in three days, and killed the prince-bishop on the steps of his own cathedral. By the end of the week the elves held better than two-thirds of Salvamont, and the surviving chapter of the [[RPG Manager/RPG Manager/Organisations_Althania's Holy Inquisition|Sixfold Inquisition]] had withdrawn to the citadel with what was left of the city's ordained.

The Fifth's chapter at Salvamont did not move, correctly: a rising is not quarry, and no Warden warrant can issue against people. **Father Edward** — Grand Warden of the Fifth until 1170 AR, and by then three days away in [[RPG Manager/RPG Manager/Locations_Valmoria_Althania_Dunvicum|Dunvicum]], holding no office, no warrant, and no authority of any kind — went anyway. Over five weeks he gathered the levies of the outlying parishes, the guild companies that had fled the city, and what could be scraped from the surrounding bishoprics. A Shepherd-Militant named Wenzel Ott sealed the muster in the field on his own authority, on the reasoning that the host existed whether or not he sealed it and an unsanctioned host is a mob. The recapture took eleven days.

The reprisals in the low quarter ran unchecked into a second month. Edward's order-of-the-day halting them survives and is dated to the ninth day; the elves of Salvamont observe that the ninth day was eight days late. Among Wardens who were present the affair is regarded uneasily to this day — as a hunt conducted against something that was never quarry, by a man who no longer had the standing to call one.

Some months later the cathedral chapter of Salvamont elected him prince-bishop.

# What actually happened
Every physical fact in the received account is true. Only the attribution and the date of one death are false, and they are false because a very small number of men decided so in a single week.

The river gates were opened from the inside, on the word of [[RPG Manager/RPG Manager/Characters_Aurelien Sarre|Prince-Bishop Aurelien Sarre]], who was half-elven, born in the low quarter, and had spent fifteen years laying petitions before the Sacred Diet about the register and the guild bar before he stopped. The garrison broke because he had disposed it to break. For five weeks he commanded the city in open revolt while the Inquisition's chapter sat besieged in the citadel knowing exactly whose city it was.

He died on the **eleventh day of the recapture**, six weeks after the account places him, armoured and unhelmed, directing the defence of the cathedral square from the cathedral steps. He was killed there by the [[RPG Manager/RPG Manager/Organisations_Band of Bob|Band of Bob]], a free company in Edward's host, who did not know whose face they had uncovered until it was over.

# The composition of the lie
The martyrdom was written in the citadel during the siege by the men Sarre had driven out of his own cathedral. Their reasoning was structural and was never much disguised. An unlawfully invested prince-bishop puts in question every ordination he performed in twenty-two years, every Oath of Chains he witnessed, and his vote in the Electoral Synod that seats the Hierarch. The [[RPG Manager/RPG Manager/Organisations_Althania's Holy Inquisition_4th Sacrament – The Arbiters of Judgment|Arbiters]] had taken his oath at investiture without examining him; the [[RPG Manager/RPG Manager/Organisations_Althania's Holy Inquisition_3rd Sacrament – The Watchers of the Threshold|Watchers]] had never drawn him. There was no version of the truth that stopped inside Salvamont's walls.

Nothing was expurgated. The [[RPG Manager/RPG Manager/Organisations_Althania's Holy Inquisition_1st Sacrament – The Keepers of Doctrine|Keepers of Doctrine]] were never asked to suppress anything, the Register of Absent Things holds no entry, and the [[RPG Manager/RPG Manager/Organisations_Althania's Holy Inquisition_6th Sacrament - The Custodians of the Black Pyre|Sixth]] — which would trade a directorate for this and knows it does not have it — has never obtained a line of it. The lie required no machinery. It was written down first, by the only witnesses anyone thought to ask, and the Realm has been reading it ever since.

# Who holds the truth
**Father Edward.** He knows, he knew when he accepted the seat, and he has never spoken of it — not at the Diet, not to the Keepers, and not to the company that did the killing. He does not use the episcopal style. Correspondence out of Salvamont is signed *Father Edward*, which the Diet has agreed to treat as an eccentricity and the Sixth reads as a message, and which is in fact neither.

**The [[RPG Manager/RPG Manager/Organisations_Band of Bob|Band of Bob]].** They killed Sarre and they know what they killed. They were decorated for a version of the deed that is adjacent to the real one, and they are the only holders of the truth whom the Realm would actually believe.

**The low quarter.** The elves of Salvamont have never regarded any of this as secret. They call Sarre *the Gatekeeper*, without affection, and their account has no documentary existence at all.

# If it came out
The see of Salvamont voids, and with it twenty-two years of ordinations out of it. The Arbiters are exposed for an unexamined oath and the Watchers for a blind draw, at the exact moment the Fourth is arguing that unreviewable orders are a danger to the Realm. Edward is finished twice over — as a man who accepted a dead heretic's seat knowing, and as the man whose reprisals answered a rising the Church had provoked itself. And **Reinhild Vahn** obtains, twenty years late, the thing the Sixth failed to get in 1170 AR.

Every one of those consequences runs through four people and a neighbourhood.
